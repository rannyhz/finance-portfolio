# Small-Business 36-Month Forecast Template

A 3-statement forecasting tool for small businesses. The owner fills in one sheet: monthly revenue, costs, payment terms, cash and debt. The workbook builds the rest:

- Income statement
- Cash flow statement
- Balance sheet
- KPI summary
- Executive dashboard

## How it works

| Sheet | Role |
|---|---|
| Control_Center | Snapshot of the forecast's key results |
| Inputs | The only sheet the user edits |
| Revenue_Cost_Engine | Applies growth, churn and payment timing |
| Income_Statement · Cash_Flow · Balance_Sheet | Linked statements that tie out |
| Forecast_Summary_KPIs | Margins, burn rate and cash runway |
| Executive_Dashboard | Charts for owners who don't read statements |

Payment terms flow through to working capital: the days until customers pay become receivables, and the days until expenses are paid become payables. That lets the cash forecast differ from profit, as it does in a real business.
